Making spatial experiences

I study how we can record, understand, and revisit experiences in augmented and virtual reality. My research connects spatial recording, scene graphs, and adaptive playback to help people navigate complex tasks.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Culture Technology at KAIST, advised by Prof. Woontack Woo.
